Course Overview

The OSHA 30-Hour General Industry course provides comprehensive training on workplace safety, hazard recognition, risk assessment and core safety responsibilities. The program is suitable for participants who need a broad, practical understanding of occupational health and safety principles.

What You Will Learn

  • Workplace hazard identification
  • Risk assessment principles
  • Emergency response fundamentals
  • Accident prevention and reporting
  • Safe work practices and responsibilities

Who Should Attend

  • Safety officers and HSE professionals
  • Engineers and supervisors
  • Managers and team leaders
  • Employees with safety responsibilities

Course Outline

  • Introduction to occupational safety
  • Walking and working surfaces
  • Electrical and fire safety basics
  • Personal protective equipment
  • Hazard communication
  • Emergency plans and incident prevention
