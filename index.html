ACCREDITATION STATUS

UAF Accreditation & Global Recognition Progress

MEERAL is progressing through its stated accreditation-development process with the United Accreditation Foundation (UAF), with the objective of aligning relevant management-system assessment and technical inspection activities with applicable international conformity-assessment requirements.

International accreditation cooperation changed in 2026: IAF and ILAC consolidated into Global Accreditation Cooperation Inc. (Global ACI). UAF states that it is internationally recognized through applicable Global ACI MLA/MRA signatory status.

Application / development status: this section intentionally states progress and does not represent MEERAL as already UAF-accredited. Formal accreditation should only be claimed after written approval and scope publication by the accreditation body.
